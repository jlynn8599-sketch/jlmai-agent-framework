The product

Security, in the repo (October 22, 2026)

  • Merkle-tree provenance — a single root hash commits to the exact contents of every file, with inclusion proofs for individual artifacts. The verifier flags missing, modified, and newly added files.
  • Canary tripwires — decoy files and tokens that should never be touched; a check reports any that are missing or changed.

Honest limits, stated plainly: a Merkle root proves integrity (files unchanged since the root was published), not authenticity on its own — that is what the GPG-signed manifest and signed commits are for. Canaries detect tampering after the fact, on the next check; they do not block anything or give real-time detection.
